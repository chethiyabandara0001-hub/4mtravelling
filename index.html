    For me…

    Our vision, mission & values

    Our name started as “Four Mi” — four family members whose names end in “mi” — and became “For Me”: every tour made just for you.

    01

    Our vision

    At 4Me Lanka Tours, our vision is to be Sri Lanka’s most trusted family run travel company, recognized worldwide for creating journeys that feel personal, seamless, and unforgettable. We aspire to showcase the Pearl of the Indian Ocean through authentic cultural immersion, warm hospitality, and tailor made itineraries that turn every guest into a lifelong friend. Our goal is to set the benchmark for sustainable, caring, and client focused tourism in Sri Lanka, where every traveler experiences the island as if it is designed “For Me”.

    02

    Our mission

    Our mission is to deliver personalized travel experiences that blend Sri Lanka’s rich heritage, breathtaking landscapes, and vibrant traditions with modern comfort and reliability. Since 2019 we have been committed to Designing Custom itineraries that reflect each traveler’s pace, interests, and dreams. Offering safe, punctual, and friendly service through experienced drivers and guides fluent in English and other languages. Coordinating Transportation, hotels, and activities with a vast network of qualified professionals across the island. by Ensuring every journey with all these, a stress free, well organized, and filled with authentic local insights we Lives with our motto “Say Hi as Guests, Bye as Friends.”

    03

    Our values

    4Me Lanka Tours reflects our family’s love for travel and creating unforgettable experiences. Our name originated from “Four Mi,” inspired by the four family members’ names ending in “mi.” As we developed our tours, we realized that each adventure was personal, leading us to the name “For Me,” highlighting our dedication to making every trip special. As a family run business, we pour our heart feelings into every detail, ensuring clients feel the tours are made just for them as for me. This connection “For Me” inspired the name 4Me Lanka Tours, capturing the essence of shared journeys in our paradise Island .
