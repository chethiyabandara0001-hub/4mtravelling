Kura’s Adventure Tour In Srilanka

12 Day Detailed Tour Itinerary

12 days Private vehicle & driver From $960

Route

NegomboKithulgalaNuwaraeliyaEllaHaputaleUdawalawaMirissaBentota

Day-by-day itinerary

  1. 1

    Arrival in BIA (Bandaranaike International Airport) to Negombo

    Travel Duration: 30 minutes to 1 hour
    +
    • Sightseeing and Activities in Negombo:
    • Negombo Lagoon: Enjoy a boat ride and bird watching.
    • Negombo Beach: Relax on the beach and participate in water sports like windsurfing and kite surfing.
    • Dutch Canal: Explore the canal by boat, offering scenic views of the town.
    • Overnight Stay: Negombo
  2. 2

    Negombo to Kitulgala

    Travel Duration: 3-4 hours
    +
    • Sightseeing and Activities in Kitulgala:
    • White Water Rafting: Experience the thrill of rafting in the Kelani River.
    • Jungle Trekking: Explore the lush rainforests around Kitulgala.
    • Caving: Discover the Belilena Cave, an archaeological site with prehistoric significance.
    • Overnight Stay: Kitulgala
  3. 3

    Kitulgala to Nuwara Eliya

    Travel Duration: 3-4 hours
    +
    • En Route Visit St. Clair’s Falls: Also known as the “Little Niagara of Sri Lanka”, Devon and Nanuoya Falls.
    • Sightseeing and Activities in Nuwara Eliya:
    • Tea Factory Tour: Learn about tea production and enjoy a tasting session.
    • Gregory Lake: Engage in boating and other water activities.
    • Overnight Stay: Nuwara Eliya
  4. 4

    Nuwara Eliya Exploration

    +
    • Sightseeing and Activities in Nuwara Eliya:
    • Horton Plains National Park: Hike to World’s End and Baker’s Falls.
    • Trekking and Hiking: Explore the various trails around Nuwara Eliya.
    • Overnight Stay: Nuwara Eliya
  5. 5

    Nuwara Eliya to Ella

    Travel Duration: 3-4 hours
    +
    • Enjoy the most famous and scenic train ride to Ella from Nanuoya.
    • Overnight Stay: Ella
  6. 6

    Ella Exploration

    +
    • Sightseeing and Activities in Ella:
    • Little Adam’s Peak: Hike to the top for stunning views.
    • Nine Arches Bridge: Visit this iconic bridge and watch trains pass by.
    • Ella Rock: A challenging hike offering panoramic views.
    • Ravana Falls: Visit the picturesque waterfall.
    • Overnight Stay: Ella
  7. 7

    Ella to Haputale

    Travel Duration: 1-1.5 hours
    +
    • Sightseeing and Activities in Haputale:
    • Lipton’s Seat: Trek to this viewpoint offering breathtaking vistas.
    • Diyaluma Falls: Visit the second-highest waterfall in Sri Lanka.
    • Overnight Stay: Haputale
  8. 8

    Haputale to Udawalawe

    Travel Duration: 2-3 hours
    +
    • Sightseeing and Activities in Udawalawe:
    • Udawalawe National Park Safari: Enjoy a safari to see elephants, leopards, and other wildlife.
    • Elephant Transit Home: Visit this facility where orphaned elephants are cared for.
    • Overnight Stay: Udawalawe
  9. 9

    Udawalawe to Mirissa

    Travel Duration: 2-3 hours
    +
    • Sightseeing and Activities in Mirissa:
    • Surfing: Enjoy surfing on the waves of Mirissa Beach.
    • Overnight Stay: Mirissa
  10. 10

    Mirissa to Bentota

    Travel Duration: 2-3 hours
    +
    • Whale Watching: Take an early morning boat trip to spot blue whales and dolphins.
    • Sightseeing and Activities in Bentota:
    • Bentota Beach: Relax and participate in water sports such as jet skiing, windsurfing, and parasailing.
    • Bentota River Safari: Explore the river and mangroves by boat.
    • Overnight Stay: Bentota
  11. 11

    Bentota Exploration

    +
    • Sightseeing and Activities in Bentota:
    • Brief Garden: Explore the beautifully landscaped garden designed by Bevis Bawa.
    • Lunuganga Estate: Visit the country home of architect Geoffrey Bawa.
    • Overnight Stay: Bentota
  12. 12

    Bentota to BIA

    Travel Duration: 2-3 hours
    +
    • Departure: Drop-off at Bandaranaike International Airport (BIA)
This detailed itinerary ensures a thrilling Adventure Tour In Srilanka with activities ranging from white water rafting to wildlife safaris, trekking, and water sports.
