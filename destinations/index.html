A whole world on one island

Tourist attractions in Sri Lanka can be classified as natural or anthropogenic. Natural attractions include nature spots, flora and fauna, and pleasant climates, while anthropogenic attractions include archaeological and cultural sites, historical landmarks, performing arts, folklore, handicrafts, and Artefacts.

The country has six botanical gardens and four Zoological gardens, attracting local and foreign visitors. Heritage tourism involves visiting historical sites, with Buddhism influencing the country’s cultural heritage. Sports tourism involves participating in specific sporting events, such as trekking, hiking, diving, rock climbing, and hot air ballooning.
